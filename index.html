Chapter 1: The Art of Quiet Discovery

Reading on a touch screen should feel natural and effortless. In ancient libraries, scholars often experienced a sudden epiphany while examining forgotten manuscripts. They scored marks into the margins of thick vellum pages, seeking patterns in the text.

Such moments of pure serendipity are rare in our noisy digital world, where internet connections flicker and distractions are ubiquitous. Looking at an antique hourglass beside a modern smartphone seemed like an amusing anachronism, yet the bell's sonorous chime reminded them that deep comprehension is never ephemeral.
